🇿🇦 South Africa — Achievements 🏆

A look at South Africa's greatest international cricket achievements.

🏆 ODI World Cup

South Africa has not yet won the Men's ODI Cricket World Cup.

⭐ Best result: Semi-finalist

🏆 T20 World Cup

South Africa has not yet won the Men's T20 World Cup.

⭐ Best result: Runner-up — 2024

🏆 Champions Trophy

South Africa won the inaugural ICC Champions Trophy in 1998.

🏆 1998 — Champions

⭐ Greatest Achievement

South Africa's greatest achievement in an ICC men's limited-overs tournament was winning the 1998 Champions Trophy.

They defeated the West Indies in the final.

🔥 2024 T20 World Cup

South Africa reached their first Men's T20 World Cup final in 2024.

🥈 They finished as runners-up after losing to India in the final.

🏏 Other Major Achievements

⭐ 1998 Champions Trophy champions

⭐ 2024 T20 World Cup runners-up

⭐ Multiple ODI World Cup semi-final appearances

⭐ One of the strongest teams in international cricket
