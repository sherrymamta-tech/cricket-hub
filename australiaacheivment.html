🇦🇺 Australia — Achievements 🏆

A look at Australia's greatest international cricket achievements.

🏆 ODI World Cup

Australia has won the Men's ODI Cricket World Cup six times.

🏆 1987
🏆 1999
🏆 2003
🏆 2007
🏆 2015
🏆 2023

🏆 T20 World Cup

Australia has won the Men's T20 World Cup once.

🏆 2021 — Australia defeated New Zealand in the final in Dubai.

🏆 Champions Trophy

Australia has won the ICC Champions Trophy twice.

🏆 2006
🏆 2009

⭐ Greatest Achievement

Australia became the first men's team to win the ODI World Cup five times when they won the 2015 tournament.

They later added a sixth ODI World Cup title by winning the 2023 World Cup.

🔥 Australia's World Cup Dominance

Australia won three consecutive Men's ODI World Cups:

🏆 1999
🏆 2003
🏆 2007

They also won the 2007 tournament without losing a match.

⭐ Two-time Champions Trophy winners
