🇮🇳 India — Achievements 🏆

A look at India's greatest international cricket achievements.

🏆 ODI World Cup

India has won the Men's ODI Cricket World Cup two times.

🏆 1983 — Captain: Kapil Dev
🏆 2011 — Captain: MS Dhoni

🏆 T20 World Cup

India has won the Men's T20 World Cup three times.

🏆 2007 — Captain: MS Dhoni
🏆 2024 — Captain: Rohit Sharma
🏆 2026 — Captain: Suryakumar Yadav

🏆 Champions Trophy

India has won the ICC Champions Trophy three times.

🏆 2002 — Shared title
🏆 2013
🏆 2025

⭐ Greatest Achievement

India became the first men's team to win three T20 World Cup titles and successfully defend the T20 World Cup in 2026.

🇮🇳 Other Major Achievements

⭐ One of the most successful teams in ICC tournaments

⭐ Won the inaugural Men's T20 World Cup in 2007

⭐ Won the 2011 ODI World Cup on home soil

⭐ Won the 2024 T20 World Cup undefeated
