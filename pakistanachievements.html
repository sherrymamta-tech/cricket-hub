🇵🇰 Pakistan — Achievements 🏆

A look at Pakistan's greatest international cricket achievements.

🏆 ODI World Cup

Pakistan has won the Men's ODI Cricket World Cup once.

🏆 1992 — Captain: Imran Khan

🏆 T20 World Cup

Pakistan has won the Men's T20 World Cup once.

🏆 2009 — Captain: Younis Khan

🏆 Champions Trophy

Pakistan has won the ICC Champions Trophy once.

🏆 2017 — Pakistan defeated India in the final.

⭐ Greatest Achievement

Pakistan's greatest ODI achievement was winning the 1992 Cricket World Cup under Imran Khan.

Pakistan defeated England in the final at the Melbourne Cricket Ground.

🔥 2017 Champions Trophy

Pakistan won the 2017 Champions Trophy in England.

🏆 Final: Pakistan defeated India by 180 runs.
