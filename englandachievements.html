🏴 England — Achievements 🏆

A look at England's greatest international cricket achievements.

🏆 ODI World Cup

England has won the Men's ODI Cricket World Cup once.

🏆 2019 — England defeated New Zealand in a dramatic final at Lord's.

🏆 T20 World Cup

England has won the Men's T20 World Cup twice.

🏆 2010
🏆 2022

🏆 Champions Trophy

England has not won the ICC Champions Trophy.

🥈 Runner-up: 2004, 2013

⭐ Greatest Achievement

England's greatest ODI achievement was winning the 2019 World Cup on home soil.

The final against New Zealand was decided by a Super Over and boundary countback.

🔥 England's T20 Success

England became the first team to win the Men's T20 World Cup twice when they won the tournament in 2022.

🏆 2010
🏆 2022

🏏 Other Major Achievements

⭐ 2019 ODI World Cup champions

⭐ Two-time Men's T20 World Cup champions

⭐ Hosted and won the 2019 ODI World Cup

⭐ One of the strongest limited-overs teams in modern cricket
