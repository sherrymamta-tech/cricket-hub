🇳🇿 New Zealand — Achievements 🏆

A look at New Zealand's greatest international cricket achievements.

🏆 ODI World Cup

New Zealand has not yet won the Men's ODI Cricket World Cup.

🥈 Runner-up: 2015 and 2019

🏆 T20 World Cup

New Zealand has not yet won the Men's T20 World Cup.

🥈 Runner-up: 2021

🏆 World Test Championship

New Zealand won the inaugural ICC World Test Championship.

🏆 2021 — New Zealand defeated India in the final.

🏆 Champions Trophy

New Zealand has won the ICC Champions Trophy once.

🏆 2000 — New Zealand defeated India in the final.

⭐ Greatest Achievement

New Zealand became the first team to win the World Test Championship in 2021.

They defeated India in the final at Southampton.

🔥 2019 World Cup

New Zealand reached the 2019 ODI World Cup final and played one of the closest finals in World Cup history.

🥈 New Zealand finished as runners-up against England.

🏏 Other Major Achievements

⭐ 2021 World Test Championship champions

⭐ 2000 Champions Trophy champions

⭐ Two-time ODI World Cup runners-up

⭐ 2021 T20 World Cup runners-up
