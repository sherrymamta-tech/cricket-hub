Rinku Singh

The journey behind the sixes

🧒 The Beginning

Before the sixes.

Before the IPL spotlight.

Before the India jersey.

There was simply a boy from Aligarh, Uttar Pradesh, who wanted to make something of himself through cricket.

Rinku Singh was born on 12 October 1997 in Aligarh. He grew up in a modest family, with his father working as a deliveryman for a gas-cylinder company.

Life wasn't surrounded by the facilities or attention that young cricketers at the highest level often have.

But Rinku kept playing.

He began making his way through Uttar Pradesh's age-group cricket, representing the state at Under-16, Under-19 and Under-23 level.

This was where the real foundation of his career was built.

There were no huge crowds.

No television cameras following every innings.

No one outside the domestic cricket world was waiting to see what he would do.

There was simply a young left-handed batter trying to prove that he belonged.

Rinku's performances eventually earned him a place in Uttar Pradesh's senior team.

In 2014, at just 16 years old, he made his List A debut.

A year later, he made his first-class debut for Uttar Pradesh.

The jump from junior cricket to senior cricket was enormous. He was now playing against experienced cricketers who had spent years competing at the highest domestic level.

But Rinku didn't disappear.

He kept producing runs.

He kept learning.

And slowly, his name began appearing more often among Uttar Pradesh's important performers.

One of his biggest domestic breakthroughs came during the 2018–19 Ranji Trophy.

Rinku had an outstanding tournament, scoring 953 runs in 10 matches, making him Uttar Pradesh's leading run-scorer that season.

That wasn't just a good tournament.

It was a statement.

He had shown that he could perform consistently over a long domestic season rather than relying on one spectacular innings.

His domestic performances helped establish him as one of Uttar Pradesh's most promising young batters.

But domestic success brings another challenge.

Getting noticed.

Rinku had the performances.

He had the talent.

But the next step would take him into a completely different world.

🏏 The Opportunity

Rinku's hard work in domestic cricket eventually brought him an opportunity that could change the direction of his career.

In 2017, he was bought by Kings XI Punjab in the IPL auction.

For a young cricketer from Uttar Pradesh, this was a huge step.

The Indian Premier League brought him into a completely different environment, surrounded by established international players and some of the biggest names in the game.

The following year, Kolkata Knight Riders signed him.

But simply being selected wasn't enough.

Rinku still had to earn his place.

He spent time learning, waiting for opportunities and working on his game.

The IPL was giving him something he couldn't get anywhere else: the chance to test himself against some of the world's best cricketers.

At the same time, his domestic performances continued to matter.

He wasn't there just to be part of the squad.

He wanted to prove that he belonged.

💔 The Setback

Rinku's journey was interrupted by injury.

He missed the 2021 IPL season, a difficult setback for a player trying to establish himself.

While he was away, the competition around him continued.

But Rinku returned to cricket and continued working towards another opportunity.

The setback didn't end his journey.

It simply meant he had to keep fighting for his place.

⏳ The Wait

Rinku began receiving more opportunities with Kolkata Knight Riders.

He started showing that he could be more than a player waiting for his chance.

He could finish innings.

He could hit powerful shots.

And he could remain calm when the pressure increased.

In the 2022 IPL season, he began getting more regular opportunities.

The signs were there.

Something was changing.

🔥 The Night That Changed Everything

9 April 2023.

Kolkata Knight Riders were facing Gujarat Titans.

KKR needed 29 runs from the final over.

Rinku Singh was at the crease.

The situation looked almost impossible.

Then came the first six.

Then another.

Then another.

Rinku hit five consecutive sixes in the final over.

KKR completed an astonishing chase with one ball remaining.

The stadium erupted.

Millions of cricket fans suddenly knew his name.

Rinku Singh.

It became one of the most unforgettable finishes in IPL history.

🇮🇳 India Calls

The IPL breakthrough opened another door.

In July 2023, Rinku was selected in India's T20I squad for the tour of Ireland.

On 18 August 2023, he made his T20 International debut against Ireland.

The player who had spent years trying to establish himself was now wearing the India jersey.

The journey from domestic cricket to international cricket had finally become real.

⭐ The International Chapter

Rinku quickly became associated with India's search for a reliable finisher.

His left-handed batting, power and ability to stay calm under pressure made him an exciting option in the middle and lower order.

During India's T20I series in South Africa later in 2023, he produced another memorable performance.

He scored his maiden T20I half-century, finishing unbeaten on 68 from 39 balls.

The international stage was no longer something he was trying to reach.

He was now playing on it.

🏆 What Makes His Story Special?

Rinku's story isn't simply about becoming an Indian cricketer.

It's about how long the road can be before people finally notice.

There were years when he was known mainly to domestic cricket followers.

There were opportunities he had to wait for.

There was an injury that interrupted his progress.

And there were moments when he had to start again.

Then came one extraordinary IPL over.

Five sixes.

One impossible chase.

And a name that suddenly became known across the cricket world.

🔥 Turning Points

1️⃣ Uttar Pradesh Cricket

Breaking through the age-group system and reaching senior domestic cricket gave Rinku the foundation for his professional career.

2️⃣ The Opportunity

His move into the IPL placed him in a completely different cricketing environment.

3️⃣ The Injury

Missing the 2021 IPL season tested his ability to return and fight for another opportunity.

4️⃣ Five Sixes

The 2023 Gujarat Titans match became the defining moment of his early IPL career.

5️⃣ India Debut

Representing India for the first time in August 2023 marked the beginning of a completely new chapter.

🇮🇳 The Journey Continues

Rinku Singh's story isn't finished.

That's what makes it exciting.

The five sixes will always be remembered.

The India debut will always be part of his journey.

But there are still more innings to play, more challenges to face and more chapters waiting to be written.

From Aligarh to the biggest stage in cricket, Rinku's journey has already shown one thing:

Sometimes the longest wait makes the moment you finally get your chance feel even bigger.
